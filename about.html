Who We Are

IKC Institute of Computer is a premier computer training institute dedicated to empowering students with practical, industry-relevant digital skills. Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between traditional education and modern technology demands, we have trained thousands of students over the years.

Our institute combines experienced instructors, modern computer labs, and a curriculum designed to meet current job market needs. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to advance your career, IKC provides the right environment for growth.

  • Industry-aligned curriculum updated regularly
  • Hands-on practical training in fully equipped labs
  • Small batch sizes for personalized attention
  • Flexible schedules for students and working professionals
  • Career guidance and placement support
